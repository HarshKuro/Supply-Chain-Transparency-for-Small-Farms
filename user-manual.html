1. Platform Architecture & Supply Chain Lifecycle

AgriTrace provides a decentralized digital trust framework connecting Australian family growers with conscious consumers. Every physical handover across the supply chain is recorded immutably in Cloud Firestore, enforcing transparent farm-gate pricing in Australian Dollars ($ AUD) and biosecurity validation.

4. Frequently Asked Questions

Why are unaccredited wholesalers restricted from verifying stock?

Australian biosecurity standards require vetted quality assurance partners. An Administrator must audit the wholesaler via Admin Portal → Wholesaler Approvals before verification capabilities unlock.

Can an Administrator revoke a wholesaler's accreditation?

Yes. The Administrator Approvals Hub provides one-click 'Revoke' actions that immediately lock the wholesaler's verification permissions if biosecurity or food standards are breached.

In what currency are platform transactions processed?

All produce pricing, order totals, farm revenues, and platform analytics are measured in Australian Dollars ($ AUD).
