1. System Overview & The 5 Roles

The Supply Chain Transparency for Small Farms (AgriTrace) platform is engineered to eliminate opaque agricultural markups and empower small Australian family farms. By linking producers directly with customers through a verifiable digital custody chain, every stakeholder has transparent oversight:

  • Farmer (Producer): Lists harvest batches, specifies AUD pricing per kg/unit, and confirms dispatch.
  • Wholesaler (Quality Auditor): Audits produce quality and verifies physical stock. Must be approved by Administrator before verifying shipments.
  • Driver (Transit Logistics): Transports batches between rural Australian regional farms and suburban/urban destinations.
  • Customer (Consumer): Purchases direct-from-farm goods in AUD and tracks real-time chain of custody.
  • Administrator (Governance): Audits platform integrity, manages stakeholders, and approves wholesaler accreditations.

2. Farmer Guide (Producers)

Farmers can easily publish fresh harvests, monitor stock, and confirm incoming customer orders.

1

Add Fresh Harvest

Navigate to My Products. Enter product name (e.g., Yarra Valley Strawberries), category, price in AUD, available quantity, and unit (kg, punnet, box).

2

Review Stock

Stock automatically updates when consumers order. You can toggle product availability or adjust inventory anytime from the products table.

3

Confirm Order

When an order arrives in Orders, click Confirm Order. This advances state to "Confirmed" so accredited wholesalers can audit the batch.

Best Practice for Farmers: Include regional harvest location details in product descriptions (e.g., "Mildura Sunraysia Region", "Coldstream Yarra Valley") to build buyer trust.

3. Wholesaler Guide (Quality Auditors)

Wholesalers bridge the gap between rural producers and metropolitan distribution by certifying that produce matches quality standards and physical quantities.

1

Registration & Approval

When you register as a Wholesaler, your account is initially placed in Pending Review status pending Admin accreditation.

2

Inspect Confirmed Orders

Go to Orders to Verify. Review the items, farmer details, destination, and produce quantities.

3

Verify Stock

Once accredited by an Admin, click Verify Stock. This updates order status to "Verified" and notifies available logistics drivers.

4. Wholesaler Approval Workflow

Regulatory Compliance Requirement: Unlike open consumers, wholesalers act as trust custodians. To prevent unauthorized verification, all wholesaler accounts must be reviewed and approved by an Administrator.

Pending Wholesalers: Unapproved wholesalers who log in will see a persistent banner: "Account Pending Administrator Approval". The "Verify Stock" button is disabled until an Admin grants accreditation.
How Admin Approves: An Administrator navigates to Admin Portal → Wholesaler Approvals (or Manage Users), locates the applicant, and clicks Approve Wholesaler. Access is unlocked immediately without page reload!

5. Logistics & Driver Guide

Logistics operators ensure cold-chain integrity and record physical custody transfers.

1

Accept Verified Shipment

Navigate to Deliveries. Filter for "Verified" orders and click Accept Delivery to assign yourself as driver.

2

Pick Up Produce

Collect harvest crates from the farm or depot, then click Mark Picked Up.

3

In Transit & Handover

Update status to In Transit during highway distribution, and click Complete Delivery upon doorstep delivery.

6. Customer Guide (Direct Consumers)

Customers enjoy fresh Australian harvest produce with full traceability of who grew it, who verified it, and who delivered it.

1

Browse Market

Explore Browse Products. View fresh Australian fruits, vegetables, and grains with farm origin names and AUD pricing.

2

Checkout & Delivery

View My Cart, review total in AUD, enter your Australian delivery address and contact phone number, and place order.

3

Live Tracking & Review

Track order state transitions from farm to door in My Orders. Leave 5-star feedback and reviews in Feedback once delivered.

7. Administrator Governance Guide

The Admin Portal provides macro-oversight of users, inventory, order volume in AUD, and regulatory accreditations.

1

Wholesaler Approvals

Go to Wholesaler Approvals. Instantly approve new applicants or revoke accreditation for non-compliant wholesalers.

2

User & Product Audits

Audit stakeholders across all Australian states in Manage Users, and inspect active harvest catalogs in All Products.

3

Financial Evaluation

Inspect project expenditure, cloud hosting costs, and farmer margin benefits in the integrated Financial Evaluation section.

9. Frequently Asked Questions

Why can't my wholesaler account verify orders immediately?

To guarantee food safety and fair certification compliance, wholesalers require Administrator verification. An administrator must approve your application via the Admin Portal → Wholesaler Approvals before verification permissions are unlocked.

In what currency are platform transactions processed?

All produce pricing, order totals, and platform volume are measured and presented in Australian Dollars ($ AUD).

How does inventory stay synchronized?

When a customer places an order, Cloud Firestore automatically decrements product quantity in real-time. If stock hits zero, the product is automatically marked as unavailable.
