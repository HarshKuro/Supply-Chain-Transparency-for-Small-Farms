1. System Overview & The 5 Roles

The Supply Chain Transparency for Small Farms (AgriTrace) platform is engineered to eliminate opaque agricultural markups and empower small Australian family farms. By linking producers directly with customers through a verifiable digital custody chain, every stakeholder has transparent oversight:

  • Farmer (Producer): Lists harvest batches, specifies AUD pricing per kg/unit, and confirms dispatch.
  • Wholesaler (Quality Auditor): Audits produce quality and verifies physical stock. Must be approved by Administrator before verifying shipments.
  • Driver (Transit Logistics): Transports batches between rural Australian regional farms and suburban/urban destinations.
  • Customer (Consumer): Purchases direct-from-farm goods in AUD and tracks real-time chain of custody.
  • Administrator (Governance): Audits platform integrity, manages stakeholders, and approves wholesaler accreditations.

3. Farmer Guide (Producers)

Farmers can easily publish fresh harvests, monitor stock, and confirm incoming customer orders.

1

Add Fresh Harvest

Navigate to My Products. Enter product name (e.g., Yarra Valley Strawberries), category, price in AUD, available quantity, and unit (kg, punnet, box).

2

Review Stock

Stock automatically updates when consumers order. You can toggle product availability or adjust inventory anytime from the products table.

3

Confirm Order

When an order arrives in Orders, click Confirm Order. This advances state to "Confirmed" so accredited wholesalers can audit the batch.

4. Wholesaler Accreditation & Audit Manual

Wholesalers bridge the gap between rural producers and metropolitan distribution by certifying that produce matches quality standards and physical quantities.

4.1 Unaccredited Wholesaler Experience (Pending Approval)

Unapproved wholesalers who log in see a persistent amber warning banner: "Account Pending Administrator Approval". The "Verify Stock" button is disabled until an Admin grants accreditation.

4.2 Accredited Wholesaler Certified Workspace

Once approved by an Administrator, the wholesaler receives certified auditing status with unlocked verification controls.

9. Frequently Asked Questions

Why can't my wholesaler account verify orders immediately?

To guarantee food safety and fair certification compliance, wholesalers require Administrator verification. An administrator must approve your application via the Admin Portal → Wholesaler Approvals before verification permissions are unlocked.

In what currency are platform transactions processed?

All produce pricing, order totals, and platform volume are measured and presented in Australian Dollars ($ AUD).

How does inventory stay synchronized?

When a customer places an order, Cloud Firestore automatically decrements product quantity in real-time. If stock hits zero, the product is automatically marked as unavailable.
