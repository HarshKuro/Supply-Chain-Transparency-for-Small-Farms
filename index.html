Core System Features

Product Management Producers list and update fresh harvests with direct pricing.
Order Tracking End-to-end transparent state transitions from farm to table.
Inventory Management Automatic real-time stock deduction upon verified customer checkout.
Delivery Status Driver assignment, transit telemetry, and direct handover verification.
Admin Governance Central oversight of all agricultural transactions, users, and compliance.
Wholesaler Accreditation Wholesalers must undergo Administrator approval before auditing farm batches.
Financial Evaluation Comprehensive CAPEX/OPEX cost analysis & farmer margin benefit modeling.
Customer Feedback Post-delivery rating and reviews stored transparently in Firestore.
