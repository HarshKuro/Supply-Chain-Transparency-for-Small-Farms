Core System Features

Product Management Producers list and update fresh harvests with direct pricing.
Order Tracking End-to-end transparent state transitions from farm to table.
Inventory Management Automatic real-time stock deduction upon verified customer checkout.
Delivery Status Driver assignment, transit telemetry, and direct handover verification.
Role-Based Access Dedicated portals for Farmer, Wholesaler, Driver, Customer, and Admin.
Customer Feedback Post-delivery rating and reviews stored immutably in Firestore.
