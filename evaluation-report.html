1. Executive Summary & Feasibility Scope

In traditional Australian agriculture, small and medium family producers frequently suffer from asymmetric pricing dictated by major supermarket duopolies (Coles and Woolworths) and speculative wholesale agents, who capture between 45% and 60% of retail shelf value. Small growers typically receive as little as 32.7% of the consumer's dollar while bearing all weather and harvest risks.

The Supply Chain Transparency for Small Farms (AgriTrace) platform solves this systemic failure through a low-overhead, serverless traceability web architecture. This evaluation analyzes the real-world expenses required to build, deploy, host, and sustain this platform under authentic Australian agricultural economics.

6. Small Family Farm Economic Benefit & Real Money Gain

Supermarket Duopoly Baseline

  • Farmer receives: $1.80 AUD per 500g punnet (32.7% of $5.50 retail)
  • Supermarket / middlemen margin: 67.3% ($3.70 AUD)
  • Delayed payment terms (45 to 60 days)
  • Up to 18% cosmetic rejection waste discarded without payment

AgriTrace Transparent Model

  • Farmer nets: $3.52 AUD per punnet (+95.5% net cash increase!)
  • Fair platform fee: 3.0% ($0.13 AUD) + local transit ($0.55 AUD)
  • Consumer pays $4.20 AUD ($1.30 cheaper than supermarket)
  • Prompt payout upon verified QR delivery
Real Money Farm Benefit:
For an average small Victorian family grower harvesting 6,000 units per season, net income rises from $10,800 AUD to $21,120 AUD, delivering +$10,320.00 AUD in extra cash profit directly into the grower's hands. Across the 15 pilot farms, over $154,800.00 AUD in collective additional rural revenue is retained locally.
