1. Executive Summary & Feasibility Scope

In traditional Australian agriculture, small and medium family producers frequently suffer from asymmetric power dynamics dominated by major supermarket duopolies and speculative middlemen, who capture between 40% and 55% of consumer produce expenditure.

The Supply Chain Transparency for Small Farms (AgriTrace) platform solves this systemic failure by deploying a low-overhead, serverless traceability web architecture. This evaluation analyzes the real-world expenses required to build, deploy, host, and maintain this platform under Australian commercial standards.

5. Economic Benefit & Return on Investment (ROI)

Traditional Duopoly Model

  • Farmer receives: 22% – 28% of shelf price
  • Middlemen markups: 45% – 52%
  • Delayed payments (up to 90 days)
  • Unsold produce waste discarded without compensation

AgriTrace Transparent Model

  • Farmer receives: 60% – 66% of customer price (+36% gain!)
  • Transparent wholesale & transit costs: ~30%
  • Direct payout upon verified delivery
  • 18% reduction in spoilage through demand-aligned harvesting
Financial Break-Even Analysis:
For an average small Australian orchard generating $180,000 AUD gross produce value, eliminating middleman exploitation yields an additional $43,200 AUD in retained annual farmer profit. A cooperative of just 15 farms generates over $640,000 AUD in collective additional income, fully amortizing the initial $75,400 AUD system development expenditure in under 7.5 months.

6. Financial Feasibility Verdict

Verdict: Highly Feasible and Economically Transformative.
Because AgriTrace leverages a modern serverless JAMstack architecture with Firebase and native ES Modules, it completely sidesteps expensive dedicated server infrastructure. The return on investment for small Australian family farms is exceptionally high, providing both a commercial competitive advantage and a resilient foundation for fair trade agricultural traceability.
