4. Strategic Feasibility for Australian Small Farms

By replacing predatory supermarket duopoly markups (where growers receive only $1.80 AUD on a $5.50 retail punnet) with direct transparent marketing, participating Australian family growers net $3.52 AUD per unit (+95.5% net margin gain). For an average small family farm harvesting 6,000 units per season, this injects +$10,320.00 AUD in extra cash profit directly into the household farm economy. Across the 15-farm pilot, over $154,800.00 AUD in collective additional income is retained locally.
