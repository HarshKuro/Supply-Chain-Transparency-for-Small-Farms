3. Strategic Feasibility for Australian Small Farms

By replacing predatory middlemen markups (typically 45% - 52%) with transparent direct-to-consumer and wholesale distribution, participating Australian farmers retain an estimated $43,200 AUD in extra income per orchard annually. For a small cooperative of 15 farms, the entire platform investment is fully recovered in just 7.5 months.
